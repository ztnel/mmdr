Keyboard help

hjkl
Pan in NORMAL; select elements directionally in VISUAL. j/k scrolls a discussion activated with c or m/M.
/ · n/N
Search elements; cycle next/previous match. Selection does not open a discussion.
c · ;c
Open/comment on the selection; comment on the whole diagram.
m/M
Cycle next/previous message globally and open its discussion.
Enter · Shift+Enter
Post a comment; insert a newline while typing. Enter or Space selects a focused diagram element.
Escape
Exit typing, search, or command mode; otherwise clear selection and close its discussion. In help, close this menu.
Arrow keys · +/− · 0
Pan the canvas; zoom in/out around its center; fit the diagram. = also zooms in. Mouse-wheel zoom follows the pointer.
:e
Re-render the diagram.
:q · :wq · :x
Close commenting, not the browser tab.
?
Open this help menu outside editable controls.
